## Brand & Style

The design system embodies high-energy neo-brutalism synthesized with modern consumer tech polish. It marries the punchy, irreverent posture of streetwear stickers and physical product tags with the precision and legibility expected of health, food, and ingredient transparency applications.

### Brand Personality
- **Energetic & Playful:** Unapologetic visual momentum driven by chunky display scales, slight rotational offsets, tactile badges, and electric color sparks.
- **Scientific Yet Approachable:** Complex data, ingredient analyses, and verification benchmarks are distilled into unmistakable grades, high-contrast badges, and tactile cards.
- **Punchy Precision:** Tactile feedback, crisp hard-edge borders, and stark chromatic pairings remove institutional friction while retaining rigorous scientific credibility.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
Geared toward modern, discerning consumers navigating ingredient safety, consumer goods, food & beverage standards, and personal wellness. The UI invokes immediate clarity, empowering confidence, and a sense of delight—transforming routine verification checks into an engaging, expressive ritual.

### Design Movement: Playful Neo-Brutalism & Kinetic Tech
The aesthetic combines heavy ink borders (`1.5px` to `2px`), offset hard shadows (`2px` to `4px` zero-blur translations), pill-shaped action surfaces, and intentional micro-rotations (`-1.5deg` to `+1.5deg`) on interactive badges and stickers.

## Colors

The system uses a deliberate hierarchy of functional ink, high-energy accents, and clean baseline surfaces.

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Canvas Base:** `#F5FDE8` (Pale Lime Tint) delivers a signature organic foundation distinct from clinical sterile whites.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White) reserved for nested brutalist cards and structural cards.
- **Primary / Brand Ink:** `#0F3822` (Deep Forest Green) delivers dense contrast for structural frames, thick card strokes, and heavy typography.
- **High-Voltage Accent:** `#D7FF63` (Electric Neon Lime) provides raw energy for primary CTA buttons, score rings, and active state fills.
- **Secondary Kinetic Accent:** `#00C6A5` (Vibrant Turquoise / Cyan) denotes successful verification, passing grades, and certified metrics.
- **Neutral Foreground:** `#121915` (Soft Charcoal Ink) used for secondary body typography, meta descriptions, and subtle outlines.

### Domain-Specific Accents
- **Food & Beverage:** Anchored in `#D7FF63` (Electric Lime) paired with `#0F3822` (Deep Forest Green).
- **Personal Care & Hygiene:** `#BCA5FF` (Soft Electric Lavender) paired with `#431A60` (Deep Plum Ink) for targeted category separation without disrupting systemic rules.

### Dark Mode Adaptation
- **Canvas Dark Base:** `#101014` (Deep Void).
- **Surface Dark Elevated:** `#191820` (Dark Charcoal Cards).
- **Dark Primary Ink / Contrast:** `#FFFFFF` and `#F5FDE8`.
- **Accents:** Electric Lime (`#D7FF63`) and Soft Lavender (`#BCA5FF`) retain luminescence against dark containers. Hard shadows shift from dark forest ink to deep black `#050507`.

## Typography

The type system creates intentional tension between **Space Grotesk** (kinetic, architectural, sharp geometric display) and **Plus Jakarta Sans** (humanist, balanced, highly readable body copy).

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Display:** Space Grotesk in heavy weights (`600`, `700`) with tight tracking delivers punchy neo-brutalist presence. Always rendered in `#0F3822` in light mode to preserve maximum punch.
- **Data & Scientific Metrics:** Ratings, numerical grades (e.g., "98/100", "GRADE A"), and verification counters utilize `metric-stat` and `label-*` tiers with uppercase styling to emphasize authority.
- **Body & Informational Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures high legibility on mobile screens for lengthy ingredient disclosures, regulatory notices, and allergen summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a dynamic column fluid grid optimized for mobile ergonomics while expanding seamlessly to tablet and desktop viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin-mobile` (16px), `gutter-sm` (12px). All primary touch elements span full width or symmetric 2-column halves.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout, `margin` (20px), `gutter` (16px).
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout with a constrained application canvas (`max-width: 1240px`), `margin-desktop` (40px), `gutter-lg` (24px).

### Spacing Rhythm
Component spacing follows a modular 4px/8px rhythm. Structural card gaps default strictly to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px) to retain breathing room despite bold borders. Micro-badges within cards use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved without soft, blurry drop shadows. Instead, the design system utilizes **Hard Offset Tactile Projection** and **Kinetic Stacking**.

### Tactile Drop Shadows (Hard Offsets)
- **Standard Card Elevation:** `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #0F3822; border: 1.5px solid #0F3822`.
- **Interactive Button / Active Elevation:** `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0F3822; border: 2px solid #0F3822`. On active/pressed states, elements shift down-right by `2px` with shadow reduced to `1px 1px 0px #0F3822`.
- **Floating Dock / Toast Elevation:** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #0F3822; border: 2px solid #0F3822`.

### Asymmetrical Tilts & Kinetic Energy
- High-priority status stickers, grade tags, and promotional flash badges receive an intentional CSS rotational tilt between `-1.5deg` and `+1.5deg` (`transform: rotate(-1deg)`).
- Tilts are restricted to small-footprint decorative or badge elements and never applied to dense body text containers.

### Dark Mode Elevation
- Drop shadows shift from `#0F3822` to pure black `#050507`.
- Borders remain stark using a lighter charcoal contrast (`#2D2B38`) or electric neon accent lines (`1.5px solid #D7FF63`).

## Shapes

The shape language balances neo-brutalist rigidity with tactile, modern consumer friendliness. 

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Cards & Data Modules:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) delivers a grounded, modern silhouette that softens the impact of bold borders.
- **Buttons & Interactive Pills:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an immediate ergonomic affordance.
- **Stickers & Micro-Tags:** `rounded-md` (8px / `0.5rem`) for compact tags, or fully circular (`50%`) for A/B/C/D grade seals.
- **Input Fields:** `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`) mirroring the card form factor.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Pill-shaped, background `#D7FF63`, color `#0F3822`, border `2px solid #0F3822`, offset shadow `3px 3px 0px #0F3822`. Space Grotesk Bold label with all-caps micro-tracking.
- **Secondary Button:** White background `#FFFFFF`, color `#0F3822`, border `2px solid #0F3822`, offset shadow `3px 3px 0px #0F3822`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive:** Ghost styling or Electric Coral `#FF5757` with hard black borders.
- **Press States:** Translation `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow reduced to `1px 1px 0px #0F3822`.

### Cards & Neo-Brutalist Containers
- Standard surface `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #0F3822` borders and `3px 3px 0px #0F3822` shadow.
- Category variants use subtle surface headers: Pale Lime (`#F5FDE8`) or Soft Lavender (`#F1ECFF`).
- Internal padding fixed to `space-md` or `space-lg`.

### Expressive Grade Stickers (A / B / C / D System)
- Circular badges (`44px` to `64px`) or skewed lozenges with `2px solid #0F3822` borders.
- **Grade A / Clean:** Background `#00C6A5` with white ink, rotated `-1.5deg`.
- **Grade B / Moderate:** Background `#D7FF63` with `#0F3822` ink, rotated `+1deg`.
- **Grade C / Caution:** Background `#FFD154` with `#0F3822` ink.
- **Grade D / Hazard:** Background `#FF5E5E` with white ink, rotated `-2deg`.

### Chips & Verification Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`.
- High-contrast background (e.g., `#BCA5FF` for clean personal care tags, `#D7FF63` for organic food tags).
- Border: `1.5px solid #0F3822`. Typography: `label-sm` in bold uppercase.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` container with `2px solid #0F3822`.
- Default shadow: `2px 2px 0px #0F3822`. Focus state expands shadow to `4px 4px 0px #00C6A5` with no blur.
- Placeholder text in `#121915` at 60% opacity.

### Lists & Scannable Rows
- Separated by `1.5px solid #0F3822` borders or individual floating pill modules with `space-xs` bottom margins.
- Trailing icons or badges use tactile circular containers with offset drop shadows.
